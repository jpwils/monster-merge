How to play

  1. Pick a monster from the shop to buy it.
  2. Drag two of the same monster together to merge them.
  3. Put monsters on the board. Front row goes in close, back rows shoot.
  4. Press Fight! Beat every enemy to win the round.
  5. Tap a monster to see what it beats. The bin gives coins back.
